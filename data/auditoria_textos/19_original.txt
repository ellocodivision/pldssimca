Nota:
El Beneficiario Controlador es la persona física o
grupo de personas físicas que:
a) Directa o por medio
de alguna persona Cliente o Usuaria obtiene, en última
instancia, el beneficio de goce, uso, disfrute,
aprovechamiento o disposición del bien o servicio
derivado de la realización de un acto u operación con
quien realice una Actividad Vulnerable o b) Ejerce el
control efectivo en última instancia de aquella persona
moral que, en su carácter de Cliente o Usuaria, lleve a
cabo actos u operaciones con quien realice una
Actividad Vulnerable, así como las personas por cuenta
de quienes celebra alguno de ellos
n this respect and in response to your request, for
purposes of section III of article 18, as well as the
provisions of articles 19 and 21 of the Federal Law for
Prevention and Identification of Transactions with
Resources of Illegal Origin (the "Anti Money-
Laundering Law"), hereby I state, under oath, that the
person whose data are indicated below shall be the
ultimate beneficiary of the Transaction and/or the Unit,

Laundering Law), and that I, the undersigned, act in
the Transaction and will act in the agreements derived
therefrom or related thereto, on behalf of the interest
and benefits of such Controlling Beneficiary.
Note:
The Beneficial Owner is the individual or group
of individuals who:
a) Directly or through a Client or
User entity ultimately obtains the benefit of enjoyment,
use, advantage, or disposal of the good or service
derived from conducting a transaction or operation
with a person carrying out a Vulnerable Activity;
or b)
Ultimately exercises effective control over the legal
entity that, as a Client or User, engages in
transactions or operations with a person carrying out
a Vulnerable Activity, as well as those individuals on
whose behalf any such transaction is conducted.
